## Summary

Set up the cargo workspace with the crates of ADR 0001, as empty crates
with docs. Write `mort-proto`: typed GUID ids, positions and their
parsing (ADR 0003), JSON-RPC request, response and notification types, and
the error shape of ADR 0002 §6.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Workspace builds and passes the CLAUDE.md verification set.
- [x] `TrackId`, `FxId`, `ItemId`, `TakeId`, `EnvelopeId`: distinct types,
      serialised as REAPER's `{GUID}` text.
- [x] Positions: `9.1`, `9.2.240`, `+4b`, `12.5s` parse. Bad input errors
      show the input, the accepted forms and an example. Conversion to
      seconds takes a tempo-map trait, tested with a constant tempo and with
      a tempo change.
- [x] RPC envelope round-trips, including `batch`. Error `data` has `kind`,
      `subject` and `fix`.

## Close-out (2026-11-18)

- The workspace has seven crates as in ADR 0001. Six are stubs that say
  which ticket fills them in. The verification set passes.
- `mort-proto`:
  - `ids`: `Guid`, plus `TrackId`, `FxId`, `ItemId`, `TakeId` and
    `EnvelopeId` generated by a macro. They parse case-insensitively and
    print uppercase.
  - `position`: `Position`, `Range` and the `TempoMap` trait, which
    mirrors REAPER's `TimeMap` functions in quarter notes.
    `SteppedTempoMap` handles step changes at bar starts and will back
    the fake session. `format_musical` converts back to text.
  - `rpc`: the envelope, `batch` as a method with `BatchParams`, error
    kinds mapped to codes -32001 to -32005, and `PROTOCOL_VERSION` 0.1.
- Deviations:
  - A bare bar (`9`) is also accepted.
  - `+Ns` (relative seconds) was added.
  - `b` means quarter notes, matching ticks. ADR 0003 §4 is updated.
- 19 tests.
