## Summary

Create MIDI items, insert, replace and read notes and CC, with positions
and lengths in musical units.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `midi.write` into a new or existing item over a range: notes
      (pitch, start, length, velocity, channel) and CC. Mode `add` or
      `replace-range`.
- [ ] `midi.read` returns notes and CC over a range.
- [ ] CLI accepts a compact note list (`C3:9.1:1b:100 E3:+1b:1b`) and a
      file (`.mid` or the same text format).
- [ ] Tests over the fake. Smoke: the notes play in REAPER at the right
      positions across a tempo change.
